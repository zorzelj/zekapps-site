BuildQuant Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

BuildQuant is published under the ZekApps brand and is designed to work primarily offline.

Your privacy

BuildQuant does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Data stored on your device

Calculation history and app settings are stored locally on your device. This local data is used only to provide features such as recent calculations, theme preferences, and default unit settings.

Accounts and online services

BuildQuant does not require an account or sign-in. The app does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or other online data collection services.

Deleting local data

Calculation history can be cleared from within the app. Other locally stored app data can be removed by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling BuildQuant.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if BuildQuant adds or changes features that affect how data is handled.
